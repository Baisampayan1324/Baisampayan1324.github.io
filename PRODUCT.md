# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tech recruiters, senior engineers, and founders scouting AI/ML talent. Context: reviewing on desktop during a hiring cycle, often comparing against other candidates' portfolios. Secondary: potential collaborators who've found a GitHub project and want to know the person behind it.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Baisampayan Dey, AI/ML developer and B.Tech student at Parul University. Goal: convert a visit into a contact, interview, or offer. Success = recruiter sends an email within one session.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic. Precision-engineered. Quietly aggressive.

Emotional goal: make the visitor feel they're looking at work that ships, not work that demos. Confident without posturing. The Lamborghini Gold accent is intentional — raw horsepower, not polish.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio (centered avatar, pastel gradients, "I love coding" bio)
- Over-animated agency site (every element flies in, nothing feels stable)
- LinkedIn-formatted resume disguised as a website

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The work is the proof. Copy supports it; it doesn't substitute for it.
2. **Cinematic restraint.** One dominant idea per viewport. Motion earns its place or it isn't there.
3. **Precision over decoration.** Every token, every spacing value, every interaction should feel engineered, not styled.
4. **Black is load-bearing.** The obsidian background is not "dark mode" — it's the material the gold reads against. Protect it.
5. **One conversion path.** Everything points toward contact. No dead ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion must be respected — the motion is heavy; users who opt out should see an equivalent non-animated version, not broken invisible content. Keyboard navigation required for all interactive elements.
